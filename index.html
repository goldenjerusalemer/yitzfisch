The Photographer

About Yitz

Yitz Fisch is a New York City photographer with a deep connection to Israel. His photographs follow its light and everyday life, finding beauty in quiet streets, passing moments and the people who give a place its soul.

From New York City to Israel, his work is drawn to the way light reveals a place and the lives within it.
