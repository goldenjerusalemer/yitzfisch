The Photographer

About Yitz

Yitz Fisch is a street photographer based in Jerusalem. His work documents the light, texture, and spirit of the city — its alleyways and markets, its prayer and its everyday motion, the moments where ancient stone meets living streets.

Every frame is shot on location in Jerusalem. Nothing staged. Just light, framed.
